About

A small retailer with a long view of towels.

Odile Home started from a simple irritation: thin, mass-market towels that were replaced every year. We went looking for textiles that get better with use, found them in family mills on Turkey's Aegean coast, and built a small retail business around bringing them to American homes at honest prices.

We are a buyer, not a manufacturer. Our job is to choose well, present each product the way its maker intended, and deliver it fast. Most of our sales are on Amazon, fulfilled by Amazon; the rest are direct through this site and to a handful of trade customers in South Florida.

How we treat a brand

We sell at or above your minimum advertised price. We use your product titles, your imagery and your packaging. We do not commingle inventory, we do not bundle without asking, and we keep a named person, not a ticket queue, on every account. If a listing of ours ever looks wrong to you, one email fixes it the same day.
