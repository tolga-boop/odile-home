What we carry

Textiles with a point of view: Turkish cotton and linen, waffle and jacquard weaves, from mills on the Aegean coast. Products that are made to be used daily and to improve with washing. We carry a focused range rather than a long tail, and we keep brand presentation intact: original packaging, correct titles and imagery, MAP respected.

How we sell

Most of our volume moves through Amazon, fulfilled by Amazon, where we hold the listing quality to the brand's standard. The rest goes direct through this site and to a small number of trade customers in South Florida: short-term rentals, spas and boutique hotels.

Why brands work with us

We pay promptly, by card or on the terms you set, we order in cases not units, we never sell below your map, and we answer the phone. Every account we open comes with a named buyer, a resale certificate, and a business address you can verify.
